# Problem Statement

<!-- Characteristics of a good problem statement
It should be:
Customer-centric – Describe the user's pain, not your product.
Specific – Avoid vague statements like "travel planning is difficult."
Evidence-backed – Supported by research, data, or observations.
Solution-agnostic – Don't mention the product or feature.
Actionable – Clear enough that teams can derive opportunities from it.
For [target user],
 who wants to [goal],
 the current experience [describe the problem],
 because [root cause].
This results in [user impact] and [business impact].
--!>
Travellers planning a vacation struggle to efficiently plan and book trips that match their preferences and budget because travel planning is fragmented across multiple platforms for research, itinerary creation, transportation, accommodation, activities, and bookings. This requires significant manual effort to compare options, coordinate decisions, and manage reservations, resulting in a time-consuming, stressful experience with decision fatigue and uncertainty about whether they have made the best choices.
